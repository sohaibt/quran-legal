Why Quran Tutors?

A calm, organised alternative to searching WhatsApp groups.

Vetted teachers

Our team reviews every teacher’s recitation and ijazah before families see them.

Free lessons every week

Every teacher offers free hours. Paid lessons have one small fixed price, paid to the teacher directly.

The right teacher

Each learner studies with a teacher of the same gender. Young boys can also learn with a female teacher.

Safe chat

Chat stays in the app. Phone numbers and contact details are hidden. Report or block any account.
